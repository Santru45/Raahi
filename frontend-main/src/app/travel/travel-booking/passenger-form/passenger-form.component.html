1. Booking & Ticketing

All bookings are subject to availability. Tickets once confirmed are non-transferable. Passengers must carry valid government-issued photo ID matching the details provided at the time of booking.

2. Cancellation & Refund Policy

Cancellations made 24 hours or more before departure are eligible for a full refund minus convenience charges. Cancellations within 24 hours attract a 25% deduction. No-shows are non-refundable.

Loyalty Coins: Earned coins from the booking will be clawed back upon cancellation. Redeemed coins will be fully refunded to your loyalty account.

3. Passenger Responsibilities

Passengers must report at the boarding point at least 30 minutes before scheduled departure for buses/trains and 2 hours for flights. The company is not responsible for missed departures.

4. Baggage & Belongings

Each passenger is allowed one check-in bag (up to 15 kg for flights, 25 kg for trains) and one cabin bag. Excess baggage charges apply. The company is not liable for loss or damage to personal belongings.

5. Travel Insurance

Travel insurance is optional but recommended. The company acts only as a booking platform and is not liable for any incidents during travel.

6. Privacy & Data Usage

Personal information provided during booking is processed in accordance with our Privacy Policy. Data may be shared with travel operators for the purpose of service fulfilment.

7. Limitation of Liability

The company shall not be liable for delays, cancellations, or route changes made by the service operator. In such cases, passengers will be eligible for refund as per the operator's policy.

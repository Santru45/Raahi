📋 Terms, Conditions & Cancellation Policy
Please read carefully before confirming
1. Booking Confirmation
  • Your booking is confirmed only after successful payment and receipt of a booking reference number.
  • The booking reference must be presented at hotel check-in along with a valid government-issued photo ID.
  • All bookings are subject to room availability at the time of payment.
2. Check-in & Check-out
  • Standard check-in time is 2:00 PM and check-out time is 12:00 PM (noon).
  • Early check-in or late check-out is subject to availability and may attract additional charges.
  • The hotel reserves the right to deny check-in if valid ID proof is not presented.
3. Payment & Pricing
  • All prices are inclusive of applicable taxes unless stated otherwise.
  • The total amount charged at the time of booking is final. No hidden charges will be levied by this platform.
  • Wallet deductions are processed immediately upon booking confirmation.
  • Loyalty coins earned will be credited to your account within 24 hours of check-out.
4. Cancellation Policy
  • Free Cancellation: Cancel at least 48 hours before check-in for a full refund (applicable only to refundable rate plans).
  • Partial Refund: Cancellations made between 24–48 hours before check-in are eligible for a 50% refund.
  • No Refund: Cancellations made within 24 hours of check-in, no-shows, or early departures are non-refundable.
  • Non-refundable rate plans are not eligible for any cancellation refund.
  • Refunds, where applicable, are processed back to the original payment source within 5–7 business days.
5. Guest Responsibilities
  • Guests are responsible for any damage caused to hotel property during their stay.
  • Smoking in non-smoking rooms may attract a cleaning fee levied directly by the hotel.
  • Pets are not permitted unless explicitly stated in the hotel's policy.
  • Guests must comply with the hotel's rules and local laws during their stay.
6. Privacy & Data
  • Your personal information including name, contact details, and Aadhaar (masked) is used solely for booking purposes.
  • Your data is encrypted and stored securely in compliance with applicable privacy laws.
  • We do not share your personal data with third parties without your consent, except as required by law.
7. Liability
  • This platform acts as an intermediary between you and the hotel. We are not liable for any service issues at the hotel property.
  • In case of disputes, the hotel's management decision shall be considered final for on-property matters.
  • Force majeure events (natural disasters, pandemics, government restrictions) may affect bookings; waivers will be assessed case-by-case.
